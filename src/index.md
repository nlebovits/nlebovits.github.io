I'm interested in helping cities use open data and new geospatial tech to manage climate risks, especially in the Global South. Lately, that mostly means I build a lot of open source software, like [Portolan](https://www.portolan-sdi.org/), [tylertoo](https://geoparquet-io.github.io/tylertoo/), and [geoparquet-io](https://geoparquet.io/). 

Currently, I'm very lucky to get to work for [Radiant Earth](https://radiant.earth/). Other cool stuff I've gotten to do includes being a Fulbright research fellow in Argentina, founding [Clean & Green Philly](https://www.cleanandgreenphilly.org/), and being the managing editor for [Symposeum Magazine](https://symposeum.us/).

Some life goals right now: getting cats, a B2 in Dutch, hiking the national parks, and writing a book that [Olga Tokarzcuk would want to read](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/literature/2018/tokarczuk/lecture/).
